Safe. Legal. Sustainable Pharmaceutical Waste Disposal.

The Drug Safety Foundation (DSF) is a specialized NGO established in 2023 with a singular goal — that all pharmacies engage in safe and sustainable pharmaceutical waste disposal. We connect community pharmacies, the FDA, and licensed incinerators in a seamless compliance network.

By aggregating expired, damaged, returned, and unwanted medicines across participating pharmacies, DSF leverages economies of scale to significantly reduce individual cost burdens, eliminate complex disposal logistics, and achieve FDA compliance without administrative burden.

Our network-level FDA coordination means better outcomes for all participants. Pooled volumes lower per-unit disposal costs for every member, while centralized incineration is cleaner and more efficient than scattered disposal.

OUR MISSION

The Drug Safety Foundation exists to ensure that all community pharmacies in Ghana engage in safe, legal, and sustainable pharmaceutical waste disposal — protecting patients, protecting communities, and protecting Ghana's ecosystem through FDA-supervised incineration and certified destruction.

"Pharmaceuticals leaching into Ghana's water bodies and soil contaminates agriculture and drinking water. DSF eliminates this risk through controlled incineration — protecting every community we serve."

Environmental Biosecurity

"An expired drug on your shelf is a risk. An expired drug in the trash is a hazard. An expired drug managed by DSF is a tax-deductible contribution to a healthier Ghana."

Financial Recovery

"Improperly disposed antibiotics allow environmental bacteria to develop resistance — fuelling the rise of superbugs. DSF removes these drugs from the waste stream entirely, fighting antimicrobial resistance at scale."
